2. What Blot is

You pick a screenshot. Blot sends it to our service, which reads the text in the image with optical character recognition, matches what it reads against patterns for email addresses, API keys and tokens, credit card numbers, phone numbers and IP addresses, and shows you what it found. You choose which findings to cover and how — a black bar, a blur, a pixel block, or decoy text that replaces the real string with a fake of the same shape — and Blot returns a cleaned PNG you can share or save.

Blot is a convenience tool that helps you spot things you might otherwise miss. It is not a compliance product, a data-loss-prevention system, a certification, or an audit. Do not treat a screenshot that has been through Blot as certified safe, and do not use Blot as your only control where a leak would be serious — for production credentials, regulated personal data, legal disclosure or anything similar.

3. No guarantee of detection

Read this one. Automated detection is imperfect. Blot can miss sensitive information in your screenshot, and it can cover the wrong thing. You are responsible for reviewing the cleaned image before you share it.

Detection depends on optical character recognition and on pattern matching, and both fail in ordinary, predictable ways. Text that is small, low-contrast, stylised, rotated, handwritten, rendered as part of an image, in an unusual script, or partly cut off may not be read at all. A secret that does not look like the patterns Blot knows — an unusual key format, an internal identifier, an account number, a home address, a face, a name, a location in a map screenshot — will not be flagged, because Blot is not looking for it. Conversely, harmless text can match a pattern and be covered when you did not want it covered.

You also control what Blot looks for. Phone numbers and IP addresses are switched off by default, and any type you turn off in Controls will not be shown or covered. A finding you leave unticked in the review screen is not covered. Blot only ever covers what you confirm.

Covering is applied to the image Blot returns, and only to that image. It does not alter, replace or protect the original screenshot in your photo library, any copy you already shared, or any other version of the image. If you share the original by mistake, Blot cannot help you.

Accordingly, and to the fullest extent permitted by law, we make no warranty that Blot will find every piece of sensitive information in any image, that a cover cannot be reversed or inferred from context, or that a cleaned image is safe to publish. The decision to share is yours, and the consequences of sharing are yours.
